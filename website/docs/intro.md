Practical notes for learning **Apache JMeter** and **performance testing**, written for trainees of the Pragmatic JMeter Training. Everything is checked against **JMeter 5.6.3**.

:::tip[Find anything fast]

Press `Ctrl + K` (`⌘ + K` on macOS) to search every page, including all JMeter components, functions and properties.

:::

The [training repository](https://github.com/pragmatictesters/Pragmatic-JMeter-Training) has ready-made test plans in `test-plans/` (API testing, recording, logic controllers, timers and more) that you can open in JMeter and follow along with.
